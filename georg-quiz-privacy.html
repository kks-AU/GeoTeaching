GEORG Quiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

About GEORG Quiz

GEORG Quiz is a geotechnical engineering learning assistant published by Aarhus University. It provides quizzes, explanations, feedback, and revision suggestions based on GeoTeaching.

For privacy questions, contact kks@cae.au.dk.

Quiz conversations and results

Aarhus University does not receive or have access to your quiz conversations, answers, or results through GEORG Quiz.

The plugin uses your selected topics, difficulty, answers, and conversation context to provide feedback and calculate results within your conversation. It has no separate quiz-results database or mechanism for sending your answers to the publisher.

Your conversation is processed by the platform where you use the plugin. Its handling, storage, access, and deletion depend on that platform’s policies, account settings, and applicable workspace arrangements.

GeoTeaching website

The GeoTeaching website does not track users and uses no analytics or cookies.

GEORG Quiz may retrieve public GeoTeaching pages. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to serve these requests under its own policies.

Support emails

If you email kks@cae.au.dk, your email address and message are received and used to respond to your enquiry.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information or confidential project data in support messages.

Privacy requests

For questions about GEORG Quiz or the handling of support emails, contact kks@cae.au.dk. You may also contact this address to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information in support correspondence; applicable retention requirements may affect deletion.

To manage or delete your quiz conversations, use the privacy controls provided by the platform where you used the plugin.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to GEORG Quiz or its data practices. A publication or revision date will appear on the published page.
