GEORG Quiz Terms of Use

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Purpose and publisher

GEORG Quiz is published by Aarhus University to support learning in geotechnical engineering. For support, contact kks@cae.au.dk.

It offers quizzes in Danish with selectable topics, difficulty, question count, and question format, followed by explanations and revision suggestions.

Educational use

GEORG Quiz provides educational assistance. Its questions, calculations, explanations, and scores may contain errors. Check relevant sources and discuss uncertainties with your instructor.

Do not rely on its output alone for engineering design, construction, safety decisions, or formal assessment. Quiz scores are practice feedback and do not constitute an official grade or qualification.

Access and charges

GEORG Quiz is intended to be available worldwide and does not involve payments or purchases. Access to the platform used to run it may be subject to that platform’s separate requirements, terms, or charges.

Responsible use

Use GEORG Quiz lawfully and follow your institution’s academic-integrity rules. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information, confidential material, or content you do not have permission to disclose.

Teaching materials and intellectual property

GeoTeaching materials and linked sources remain subject to their applicable licences and rights notices. These terms do not grant rights to reproduce textbook pages, figures, tables, or other third-party material.

GEORG Quiz does not replace Geoteknik, 3. udgave or other required course materials.

Availability and changes

Features and source material may change, and access may sometimes be interrupted. Source coverage may be incomplete; the assistant should explain when it cannot substantiate an answer.

Other applicable terms

Your use of the underlying platform remains subject to its applicable terms. These terms do not restrict rights that cannot lawfully be restricted.
