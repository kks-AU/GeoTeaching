GEORG_V2 Terms of Use

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Purpose and publisher

GEORG_V2 is published by Aarhus University to support learning in geotechnical engineering. For support, contact kks@cae.au.dk.

It offers tutoring in Danish, including step-by-step explanations, worked examples with intermediate calculations and units, practice exercises, and feedback on your approach.

Educational use

GEORG_V2 provides educational assistance. Its questions, calculations, explanations, and feedback may contain errors. Check relevant sources and discuss uncertainties with your instructor.

Do not rely on its output alone for engineering design, construction, safety decisions, or formal assessment. Exercise feedback does not constitute an official grade or qualification.

Access and charges

GEORG_V2 is intended to be available worldwide and does not involve payments or purchases. Access to the platform used to run it may be subject to that platform’s separate requirements, terms, or charges.

Responsible use

Use GEORG_V2 lawfully and follow your institution’s academic-integrity rules. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information, confidential material, or content you do not have permission to disclose.

GEORG_V2 does not replace Geoteknik, 3. udgave or other required course materials.

Availability and changes

Features and source material may change, and access may sometimes be interrupted. Source coverage may be incomplete; the assistant should explain when it cannot substantiate an answer.
